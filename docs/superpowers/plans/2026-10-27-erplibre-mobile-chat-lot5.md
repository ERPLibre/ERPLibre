# ERPLibre mobile chat, lot 5 (finding) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** From the phone, find a message, a conversation, a person or a
file (on the phone first, offline, then completed by the server), keep
chosen conversations on top of the list, see and set the pinned messages
of a channel and one's starred messages, and use Odoo's inbox (mentions,
followed records, assignments): mark one or all read, open the item (the
app's note when one is linked, else the record), reply in the record's
chatter with everything a conversation message holds (text, mentions,
emoji, photos, files, voice), as a note by default; everything queued
offline, never applied twice, and never marking anything read behind the
user's back.

**Architecture:** A folded plain-text column on `chat_messages`, filled at
the one write point (`messageRow`), queried with `LIKE` through
`rawQuery` (an FTS5 trigram index over the same column when the phone's
SQLCipher has it, same result set); a `chat_mailbox` cache table holds every
message the inbox, history and starred boxes show, of any model; a
`chat_on_top` table holds the conversations pinned to the top of the list,
local to the phone, in the order a setting chooses (pinned first, or unread
first). Five new outbox kinds: `chat.pin` (a message action, target state),
`chat.star`, `chat.inbox_done`, `chat.inbox_all` (account actions, a new
runner), and `chat.record_post` (a post to a record with its uploads,
deduplicated like channel posts; the upload ops gain a record thread). The
reply composer on an inbox item reuses the conversation's composer pieces.
The server module 18.0.1.4.0 adds a member-only search route that never
marks read, an idempotent star setter, the client-uuid dedup on every
`mail.thread`, its idempotent upload route opened to any record the user
may post on, and a level field whose selection names the level; without it
the app searches with `search_read`, re-reads the star before toggling it,
and finds a lost reply or upload by its marker or its pending attachment.

**Tech Stack:** as lots 1–4 (TypeScript, Owl 2, Capacitor 8, SQLite over
SQLCipher, Vitest; Odoo 18 module in Python; ERPLibre long tests and device
runner).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-erplibre-mobile-chat-design.md`

## Review Focus

1. **A term with `&`, `<`, `'`, `"`, `%`, `_`, an accent or two words (« l'eau & co », « 100% », « café crème »):** the phone and the server find the same messages; `%` and `_` match only themselves; « cafe » finds « café » on the phone (Task 1 `test_search_escapes_entities_and_wildcards`; Task 6 `a term with % matches only a literal percent`, `folded term finds accented text`; Task 7 `the fallback domain escapes like the module`).
2. **A search hit older than everything loaded, opened offline then online:** offline it says the message is not on the phone yet and opens at the bottom; online it pages back with `around`, opens at the message, highlights it, and marks nothing read (Task 7 `open at an unloaded hit pages with around and marks nothing read`, `open at a hit offline shows the notice`; Task 15 long test).
3. **A star tapped on a message starred in the browser since the last load, or tapped twice offline then sent with the answer lost:** the server ends on the last tap, never on its inverse (Task 9 `without the module a stale base re-reads before toggling`, `a replayed star after a lost answer sends no second toggle`; Task 15 long test).
4. **« Tout marquer comme lu » tapped offline while new mentions arrive before the network returns:** the items newer than the tap stay unread, on the server and in the tile count, and the tile drops exactly once per item (Task 10 `mark all is bounded by the newest item shown`, `a done item and its bus echo lower the count once`; Task 15 long test).
5. **A reply with a photo on a record the user may read but not post on, or whose answer is lost on the way:** refused with a notice that keeps the text and the file; after a lost answer, one message with one attachment, and no email for a note (Task 1 `test_upload_on_record_respects_post_access`; Task 12 `a reply refused for access keeps its text and files`, `a retried reply with a photo posts once with one attachment`; Task 15 long tests).
